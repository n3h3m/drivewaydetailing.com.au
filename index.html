FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to supply water or power?

No · our van carries its own water tank and battery power. We just need a safe spot to park near your car.

How long does each package take?

Basic Refresh: ~60 min. Premium Detail: ~2 hours. Full Interior: ~2.5 hours. Showroom Detail: ~4–5 hours. Times vary with vehicle size and condition.

What if my car is heavily soiled?

Send us a photo or two when you book and we'll quote upfront. Tradies' utes, family SUVs, post-renovation cars · we've seen it all.

How do I pay?

Cash or bank transfer on the day. Card payments available on request · let us know when booking.

Do you do regular weekly or fortnightly cleans?

Yes · the Basic Refresh ($79 sedan / $99 SUV) is built for it. Ask about our recurring slots when you book.

What areas do you cover?

Sydney metro & surrounding suburbs · see the Service Areas section. Outside that zone, a small fuel surcharge may apply.
