FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to supply power or water?

Yes · we just need access to power and water within 20 metres of your car, plus a safe spot to park nearby.

How long does each package take?

It depends on the package, your vehicle’s size and its condition. Silver is the quickest and Diamond takes the longest · we’ll give you a time estimate when we confirm your booking.

What if my car is heavily soiled?

Send us a photo or two when you book and we'll quote upfront. Tradies' utes, family SUVs, post-renovation cars · we've seen it all.

How do I pay?

All payment methods are accepted, including card payments.

Do you do regular weekly or fortnightly cleans?

Yes · the Silver Basic Detail (from $129) is built for regular maintenance. Ask about recurring slots when you book.

What areas do you cover?

Sydney metro & surrounding suburbs · see the Service Areas section. Outside that zone, a small fuel surcharge may apply.
