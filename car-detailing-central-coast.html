Coastal living is hard on a car

The Central Coast sits north of Sydney along the M1 Pacific Motorway, with towns spread around Brisbane Water and the beaches. Beach days mean sand in the carpets and boot, and wet towels and wetsuits do the upholstery no favours. A regular Silver Basic Detail keeps sand and grime under control, while Carpet & Floor Mat Shampoo (from $50) and the Gold Standard Detail lift the build-up that a vacuum alone leaves behind.

Paint that has seen a lot of sun

Cars that live outdoors near the coast can lose their gloss over time. A Cut & Polish (from $349) is a two-stage machine paint correction that brings back depth and shine, and the Diamond Ultimate Detail combines it with a full interior detail, ozone treatment and headlight restoration. If you are planning to sell, the Platinum Pre-Sale Detail is designed to lift presentation before a car goes on the market.

Before we arrive

  • Power and water: we need access to power and water within 20 metres of your car, plus a safe spot to park nearby.
  • Clear out your belongings: removing valuables and loose items from the cabin and boot means we can reach and clean every surface.
  • Tell us about problem areas: pet hair, stains, odours or heavy soiling. Add a note or a photo when you book so we can quote accurately.
  • Timing: how long a detail takes depends on the package, your vehicle’s size and its condition. We give you a time estimate when we confirm your booking.

Payment: all payment methods are accepted, including card payments. Most enquiries get a reply within the hour during business hours (Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM).
